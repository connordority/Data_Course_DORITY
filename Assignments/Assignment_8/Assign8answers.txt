1. Are any of your predicted response values from your best model scientifically meaningless? Explain.

Some predicted values could be scientifically meaningless if they fall outside a realistic biological range. For example, if the model predicts a negative fungal growth rate, that may not make biological sense. Predictions may also be less meaningful when they are made for combinations of temperature, light, nitrogen, humidity, and species values that were not well represented in the original dataset.

One way to handle non-linear relationships in R is to use polynomial terms or more flexible models, such as generalized additive models (GAMs), which let the data determine the shape of the relationship rather than forcing a straight line.
